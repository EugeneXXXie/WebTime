TRACKING

All open, loaded websites are counted, including background tabs and windows. Keyboard inactivity and window focus do not pause tracking.

All subdomains of the same main domain count once. Different websites can overlap; total browser time counts each moment once. Close tabs you no longer want to count.

Locked screens, private windows, internal pages, and tabs discarded or frozen by the browser are excluded.
